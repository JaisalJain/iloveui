The atelier · Lyon

Ninety-one steps, four pairs of hands.

Every No. 31 passes the same bench on the Quai Saint-Vincent. Three of the steps decide how it writes.

  1. 01

    Turned

    Each barrel is cut from a single rod of ebonite, rested for a week, then turned again to size.

    Time at the bench: 3 h 10 min

  2. 02

    Ground

    The tipping is shaped on a brass wheel to one of five grades and the slit opened by hand.

    Time at the bench: 48 min

  3. 03

    Tuned

    The tuner writes on three papers, adjusts the flow and signs the test sheet that ships with the pen.

    Time at the bench: 35 min
