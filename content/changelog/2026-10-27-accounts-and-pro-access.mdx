### Accounts

- Create an account and sign in. Your account shows your license, purchase date and invoice, your CLI token and your bookmarks.
- Bookmark designs from any design page and find them again on your account.

### Pro and Team

- One lifetime payment unlocks every current and future design. The pricing page showed Free, Pro and Team, in rupees in India and US dollars elsewhere.
- Dodo Payments handles checkout, sales tax, VAT and invoices.
- Team owners invite up to nine more people by email; an invite becomes a seat when that person signs in.
- A refund ends access at once, CLI tokens included.

### Code access

- Pro code tabs, the AI prompt and downloads unlock on design pages as soon as the license is active.
- Create a token on your account and install any design with `npx shadcn@latest add @iloveui/<slug>`. The [CLI docs](/docs/cli) cover setup and every error.
- Downloads are zips with `component.tsx`, `component.html`, `ai-prompt.md`, `LICENSE.txt` and a `theme.css` built from your Theme Lab values.
