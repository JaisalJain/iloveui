## What you can do

- Use the designs you have access to in **unlimited personal and commercial end products**, for yourself and for your clients.
- Change them as much as you like and combine them with your own work.
- Ship them in websites, web apps, desktop and mobile apps, internal tools and client deliverables.

## What you cannot do

- Resell, sublicense or redistribute the designs, **original or modified**, as a UI kit, template, component library or design asset.

In short: build products with them, and do not turn them into a product of their own.

## Free, Pro and Team

- **Free** designs follow the same terms at no cost.
- **Pro** is one lifetime license for one person: every current design and every design added later.
- **Team** covers up to 10 people, each with their own seat, managed by the owner from their account.
